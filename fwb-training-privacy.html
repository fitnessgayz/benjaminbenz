GRUNTR Privacy Policy

Effective November 26, 2026. GRUNTR does not sell personal information, show third-party advertising, or track clients across other companies' apps or websites.

Who this policy covers

GRUNTR is the client web app operated by Fitness with Benjamin. Authorized coaching clients can also use the companion iOS app. This policy explains how information is handled when a client uses either experience.

Information we process

Depending on the features a client chooses to use, GRUNTR may process:

  • Account information: email address, account identifier, and the name or profile information connected to the coaching account.
  • Fitness questionnaire information: the name and email address supplied with the questionnaire, contact and scheduling details, fitness goals, training history, lifestyle information, readiness answers, and health, pain, injury, or surgery information a person chooses to provide.
  • Training and fitness information: assigned programs, saved generated workout plans, planned workout dates, custom workouts, exercise names, sets, repetitions, weight, effort, timers, cardio entries, workout history, and personal records.
  • Health and progress information: readiness and weekly check-ins, pain or limitation notes, body weight, body-fat percentage, muscle mass, DEXA lean mass, body measurements, progress notes, and calorie or macro targets.
  • User content: DEXA scan reports, food barcodes and food-label photos a client chooses to scan or upload, profile photos, progress photos, form-check photos or videos, workout notes, form-check notes, private workout comments exchanged with the coach, and optional gym reviews and photos.
  • Notification information: notification preferences and an app-specific device token used to deliver notifications.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate clients, display assigned coaching programs, save and synchronize workouts and planned workout details across signed-in devices, show planned workouts to the authorized coach, recover unfinished workouts, calculate and show progress, propose measurements from an uploaded DEXA report for client review, retrieve nutrition facts from an optional food barcode or food-label photo, provide readiness and macro features, deliver requested notifications, support coach feedback, maintain app security, and respond to support or privacy requests. It is not used for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

Optional GRUNTR connection in ChatGPT

When a client connects GRUNTR in ChatGPT, the plugin uses the client's authorized FWB account connection. In response to a client's request, GRUNTR shares relevant information with ChatGPT, such as the connected display name and masked email, workout history and drafts, exercise and set details, and assigned or saved routines. Training suggestions may also use available energy, soreness, or sleep duration from FWB training check-ins and energy from workout feedback.

When the client asks to log a performed set or completed workout, GRUNTR stores the supplied information in the client's FWB account. This can include dates, titles, exercises, repetitions, loads and units, bodyweight sets, optional effort ratings, and notes. Completed workouts are available in the same iOS and web history and to the authorized coach under the account's existing permissions. GRUNTR stores request IDs and receipts to prevent duplicate saves. Routine previews can be approved for 30 minutes and require the client's approval in the signed-in GRUNTR dashboard. GRUNTR clears the proposal details after approval or, for an expired proposal, the next time the client requests a preview. Approved routines are saved in the client's FWB account.

OpenAI handles ChatGPT conversations under the terms and privacy settings of the client's ChatGPT account. Disconnecting the plugin stops future access. Workout and routine records already saved to the FWB account follow the retention and privacy-request process below.

Storage and service providers

Authentication, coaching records, synchronized workout data, private media, uploaded DEXA reports, notifications, and authenticated client copies of fitness questionnaires are handled through the Fitness with Benjamin Supabase project. Supabase acts as a service provider for database, authentication, private file storage, and related infrastructure. Questionnaire submissions are also sent through Google Apps Script to a restricted Google Sheet used by Fitness with Benjamin to review responses and connect them to the correct coaching client. A questionnaire is linked to a signed-in client profile only when its email address exactly matches the verified account email. Limited preferences and unfinished workout data may also be stored on the client’s device for settings, timers, offline recovery, and synchronization.

When a questionnaire is linked to a client account, selected answers may be used to fill profile fields that are still blank, such as name, phone number, fitness goal, or available home equipment. Existing client or coach-entered profile information is not replaced automatically.

If a client chooses automatic DEXA import, the uploaded report is sent to OpenAI to extract a proposed scan date, body weight, body-fat percentage, and lean mass. OpenAI processes the report as a service provider under its applicable API terms and data-retention practices. Automatic extraction can be wrong, so the proposed values are shown to the client for review and editing before they are saved to the progress profile. The feature is optional; clients may enter measurements manually instead.

If a client chooses food-label scanning, the selected photo is sent to OpenAI to propose the product name, serving, calories, protein, carbohydrates, and fat. The photo is processed transiently and is not retained in the food library. Automatic extraction can be wrong, so the values are shown for review and editing before the food log is saved. After confirmation, the reviewed food details—not the photo—are added to a shared food-label library available to authenticated clients.

If a client chooses barcode scanning, the barcode number is checked against the shared food library and may be sent to Open Food Facts to retrieve product and nutrition details. Open Food Facts provides its database under the Open Database License (ODbL). The retrieved values are shown for review and editing before the food log is saved. After confirmation, the reviewed barcode and food details may be added to the shared food library for other authenticated clients.

Profile photos sync between the web and iOS apps for the same account. Clients can choose to display their profile photo or an emoji in Social. DEXA reports, progress media, and form-check media are stored in private storage and accessed through time-limited links. Exercise demonstration links may open YouTube outside the app. Google and YouTube’s terms and privacy practices apply when viewing those links.

The iPhone app uses Sentry to diagnose crashes, app errors, and slow screens. It sends limited diagnostics without attaching screenshots, session replays, or workout records.

Clients can import workout-history CSV files exported from Fitbod or Hevy. The selected file is parsed on the client’s device; the file itself is not uploaded. The client reviews the set count before saving. Imported exercise names, dates, sets, repetitions, weights, and optional notes are saved to the FWB account and are available to the client and authorized coach like other workout logs. Imports do not create gym check-ins or Community posts.

Nearby gym search is optional and requests location permission only when the client searches. The search sends a rounded location to OpenStreetMap’s Overpass service to retrieve public gym and hotel-gym place data; a rounded search area is cached for up to 24 hours. The exact device search location is not saved. If a client chooses to add a missing place at their current location, that place’s coordinates and name become visible to signed-in clients. Gym reviews and uploaded gym photos are visible to signed-in FWB clients with the author’s Community nickname and avatar, without displaying their email or invite code. Photos are re-encoded before upload and stored in private storage with time-limited links. Clients can edit or delete their own review and photos.

Social sharing

Clients with a Community profile appear in the weekly Leaderboard by default and can turn off ranking visibility from Social. Rankings can display the Community nickname, avatar, and weekly totals for workouts, gym check-ins, personal records, and badges earned. Feed sharing choices separately control whether other signed-in clients can see activity such as shared workout summaries. A shared summary can include the workout title, duration, sets, volume, and an exercise preview.

Community reports and blocks are private. GRUNTR uses account identifiers, the report reason, and any details you provide to review reports, apply blocks, and moderate Community content. GRUNTR reviews photos shared in Community before other members can see them.

Spotter

Spotter is an optional feature for members age 18 and older. If you activate a Spotter profile, GRUNTR stores your profile choices and records your age confirmation and waiver acceptance. The training ZIP you enter is used to find nearby members and is kept private, along with your gym name. Other eligible Spotter members can see your display name, optional About text, and selected training and partner preferences. Spotter uses approximate ZIP-area locations for radius searches.

Your Spotter profile is separate from your main GRUNTR profile. You can add a profile photo and three additional photos. Photos are held for review before they appear to other members. Body type, current program, and current supplements are optional profile fields. You can also enter your height and weight. Height and weight stay private unless you choose to share them with accepted connections. Training stats are shared only when you select specific values to share, and only with accepted connections.

Connection requests and messages are stored with your GRUNTR account. A chat opens after a member accepts a connection request. If you send a report, authorized moderators may review your report and an excerpt of the reported content. You can edit or pause your profile in Spotter. Pausing hides your profile and suspends connections and chat while paused. To remove your Spotter data, use GRUNTR’s account deletion option or contact us for help.

Apple Health

Apple Health integration is optional. If a client grants permission, GRUNTR writes completed workout type, start and end time, duration, and any distance or calories the client entered. Clients may separately allow FWB to read selected workouts (type, dates, duration, active calories, distance and average heart rate), daily steps, sleep duration, resting heart rate, heart-rate variability and body weight. Readings are shown on the iPhone. A separate, initially off choice for each category allows the last 30 days of selected records, dates, source app and record identifiers to sync to the existing FWB account service, where the client and authorized coach can view them on the web. Turning off a sharing category deletes its imported FWB data without changing Apple Health records or manual FWB logs. Sync runs when FWB is open or active. Clients can optionally enable automatic workout import, which lets Apple Health notify FWB of completed workouts in the background and refresh selected readings. iOS controls delivery and may delay it; opening FWB retries unfinished syncs. Imported workouts appear in Logs with their source, and account or coach sharing still requires the separate category choice. Clients can separately enable on-device workout detection alerts. The notification uses generic text without workout measurements; FWB stores per-account notification preferences, the opt-in time and workout identifiers on the device to avoid repeat alerts. This does not detect exercise from raw motion or provide continuous background monitoring. The Watch can optionally display live heart rate; its measurement session is discarded to avoid duplicating the workout saved by the iPhone. Health data is never used for advertising, marketing, or data mining, and is not sold or shared with data brokers. Apple Health access can be changed at any time in iPhone Settings.

Google Health, Fitbit, and Pixel Watch

Google Health connectivity is optional and requires the client’s consent in Settings. When enabled, GRUNTR reads available exercise records from the connected Google Health account, including Fitbit and Pixel Watch workouts. These records can include activity type, workout date, start and end times, duration, total calories, distance, and average heart rate. The records are stored in the Fitness with Benjamin Supabase project and displayed in Saved Logs to the client and their authorized coach. GRUNTR does not send workouts back to Google through this connection.

Automatic sync checks the most recent 30 days of exercise records approximately every 15 minutes. Clients can pause automatic sync, sync on demand, or disconnect in Settings. Disconnecting stops future imports and removes FWB’s stored connection credentials; previously imported workout records remain in Saved Logs unless deletion is requested. OAuth credentials are stored server-side and are not exposed in the client app. Google may require the client to reconnect if consent expires or is revoked.

Google Health data is not used for advertising, marketing, credit decisions, data mining, or training AI models, and is not sold or shared with data brokers. GRUNTR’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. A client can also revoke access from the security settings of the connected Google Account or contact us to request deletion.

Retention and deletion

To permanently remove your GRUNTR account and its stored records, open Settings in the iPhone app and choose Delete account under Account. If you submitted a fitness questionnaire, GRUNTR keeps your verified email in a private follow-up queue while it checks for copies stored outside the app. GRUNTR will email you after that check. If you use the web app or need help signing in, contact fwb@benjaminbenz.com for help with account deletion, access, corrections, or an export.

Coaching records are retained while needed to provide the service, maintain legitimate business records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Retention may vary by record type. We may verify requests before completing them. Data that must be retained for legal, security, or fraud-prevention reasons may be kept only for the required period.

Security and choices

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect client information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Clients can decline Apple Health access, pause or disconnect Google Health in Settings, disable notifications, choose manual entry instead of automatic DEXA or food-label extraction, change or remove their profile photo in Settings, remove uploaded progress photos within the app where available, sign out, or contact us about their information.

Children

GRUNTR is not directed to children under 13. If information from a child under 13 was provided without appropriate authorization, contact us so it can be reviewed and removed.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to fwb@benjaminbenz.com.

GRUNTR is not an emergency or medical service. For immediate danger or urgent medical concerns, contact the appropriate local emergency or medical service.
