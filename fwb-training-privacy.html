FWB Training Privacy Policy

Effective August 25, 2026. FWB Training does not sell personal information, show third-party advertising, or track clients across other companies' apps or websites.

Who this policy covers

FWB Training is a client service operated by Fitness with Benjamin. It is available to authorized coaching clients through the iOS app and Fitness with Benjamin web portal. This policy explains how information is handled when a client uses either experience.

Information we process

Depending on the features a client chooses to use, FWB Training may process:

  • Account information: email address, account identifier, and the name or profile information connected to the coaching account.
  • Training and fitness information: assigned programs, custom workouts, exercise names, sets, repetitions, weight, effort, timers, cardio entries, workout history, and personal records.
  • Health and progress information: readiness and weekly check-ins, pain or limitation notes, body weight, body-fat percentage, muscle mass, body measurements, progress notes, and calorie or macro targets.
  • User content: progress photos, form-check photos or videos, workout notes, form-check notes, and private workout comments exchanged with the coach.
  • Notification information: notification preferences and an app-specific device token used to deliver notifications.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate clients, display assigned coaching programs, save and synchronize workouts, recover unfinished workouts, calculate and show progress, provide readiness and macro features, deliver requested notifications, support coach feedback, maintain app security, and respond to support or privacy requests. It is not used for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

Storage and service providers

Authentication, coaching records, synchronized workout data, private media, and notifications are handled through the Fitness with Benjamin Supabase project. Supabase acts as a service provider for database, authentication, storage, and related infrastructure. Limited preferences and unfinished workout data may also be stored on the client’s device for settings, timers, offline recovery, and synchronization.

Progress and form-check media are stored in private storage and accessed through time-limited links. Exercise demonstration links may open YouTube outside the app; use of YouTube is governed by Google and YouTube’s own terms and privacy practices.

Apple Health

Apple Health integration is optional. If a client grants permission, FWB Training writes completed workout type, start and end time, duration, and any distance or calories the client entered. FWB Training does not read information from Apple Health. Health data is never used for advertising, marketing, or data mining, and is not sold or shared with data brokers. Apple Health access can be changed at any time in iPhone Settings.

Google Health and Fitbit

Google Health integration is optional and write-only. If a client chooses to connect it, FWB Training writes completed workout type, title, start and end time, duration, and any calories entered with the workout. FWB Training does not read the client’s existing Google Health or Fitbit data. OAuth access and refresh tokens are encrypted and stored server-side in the Fitness with Benjamin Supabase project; they are used only to perform the workout synchronization requested by the client.

Google Health data is not used for advertising, marketing, credit decisions, or data mining, and is not sold or shared with data brokers. FWB Training’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. A client can disconnect Google Health in the web portal and can revoke access from the security settings of the connected Google Account.

Retention and deletion

Coaching records are retained while needed to provide the service, maintain legitimate business records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Retention may vary by record type. A client may request access, correction, export, or deletion of their account and associated personal data by emailing fwb@benjaminbenz.com. We may verify the request before completing it. Data that must be retained for legal, security, or fraud-prevention reasons may be kept only for the required period.

Security and choices

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect client information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Clients can decline Apple Health access, disconnect or revoke Google Health access, disable notifications, remove uploaded progress photos within the app where available, sign out, or contact us about their information.

Children

FWB Training is not directed to children under 13. If information from a child under 13 was provided without appropriate authorization, contact us so it can be reviewed and removed.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to fwb@benjaminbenz.com.

FWB Training is not an emergency or medical service. For immediate danger or urgent medical concerns, contact the appropriate local emergency or medical service.
