FWB Training Client ChatGPT Plugin Terms

Effective November 2, 2026.

Who can use the plugin

The plugin is for authorized Fitness with Benjamin clients who connect their own FWB Training Client account to ChatGPT. Keep your account credentials private and use the plugin only with information you are entitled to provide.

Workout logging

When you explicitly ask the plugin to log a completed workout, it saves the exercise, set, repetition, weight, date, and note details you provide to your connected FWB account. Review the details before requesting a save. A saved workout may appear in your FWB Training Client iOS and web Logs and may be visible to your authorized coach under your existing account permissions. Contact support if a saved record needs correction or deletion.

Limitations

ChatGPT can misunderstand a request or make a mistake. The plugin does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, emergency response, or a substitute for professional care. Stop exercising and seek appropriate care if you have a concerning symptom or injury.

Service and contact

Plugin availability may change as the service is maintained. Your use of ChatGPT is also subject to the terms of the ChatGPT account you use. For support or questions about FWB records, email fwb@benjaminbenz.com.
