GRUNTR ChatGPT Plugin Terms

Effective November 2, 2026.

Who can use the plugin

The plugin is for authorized Fitness with Benjamin clients who connect their own GRUNTR account to ChatGPT. Keep your account credentials private and use the plugin only with information you are entitled to provide.

Workout logging and history

You can ask the plugin to show your workout history or log a performed set or completed workout. Review the exercise, repetitions, load, date, and any optional effort rating or notes before requesting a save. Finished workouts appear in your GRUNTR iOS and web Logs and may be visible to your authorized coach under your existing account permissions. Contact support if a saved record needs correction or deletion.

Training suggestions and routine approval

Progression suggestions are training targets based on the available workout and recovery information. You can preview a new or revised routine in ChatGPT. To save it, review the details and approve the proposal in your signed-in GRUNTR dashboard within 30 minutes. Your approval saves a client-owned routine. Your coach manages any coach-assigned program separately.

Limitations

ChatGPT can misunderstand a request or make a mistake. The plugin does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, emergency response, or a substitute for professional care. Stop exercising and seek appropriate care if you have a concerning symptom or injury.

Service and contact

Plugin availability may change as the service is maintained. Your use of ChatGPT is also subject to the terms of the ChatGPT account you use. For support or questions about FWB records, email fwb@benjaminbenz.com.
