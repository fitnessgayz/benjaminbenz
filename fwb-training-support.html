FWB Training Support

Need help? Email fwb@benjaminbenz.com. Include the email address on your FWB account and a short description of the issue. Never email your password.

Sign-in help

FWB Training requires an active Fitness with Benjamin client account. Use the same email and password as the client web portal. If a password is forgotten, use the reset-password option on the sign-in screen. Contact support if the account or assigned program is missing.

Workout and progress syncing

Workout logs, assigned programs, measurements, progress photos, and supported check-ins synchronize with the web portal through the same client account. If a workout was completed offline, keep the app installed and reopen it when the phone has a connection so pending progress can synchronize.

Apple Health

Apple Health is optional and write-only. When enabled in Workout Settings, completed workouts can be saved to Apple Health. Permission can be reviewed or disabled in iPhone Settings without affecting normal workout logging.

Google Health and Fitbit

Google Health is optional and write-only. Connect it from the client web portal to save completed workouts to Google Health and supported Fitbit devices. Disconnecting it does not affect normal workout logging. Access can also be revoked from the security settings of the connected Google Account.

Notifications and media

Workout reminders and other FWB notifications can be changed in the app’s Preferences screen or in iPhone Settings. Progress photos and form-check photos or videos are optional. Only upload media you are authorized to share.

Request account deletion

Email fwb@benjaminbenz.com with the subject “FWB Training account deletion request.” We may ask the account owner to verify the request. Deletion includes the account and associated personal data unless specific information must be retained for legal, security, or fraud-prevention reasons.

Privacy

Read the FWB Training Privacy Policy for details about data handling and privacy choices.
