FWB Training Assistant Privacy Notice

Effective August 12, 2026.

The assistant accesses only the coaching account linked by the user. Depending on the request, it may retrieve coaching goals, program summaries, workout logs, progress entries, check-ins, and open messages to Benjamin.

The assistant does not intentionally store complete ChatGPT or Claude conversations. It stores a workout log, structured check-in, progress note, or coach request only when the user asks to save or send it. Explicit correction and undo requests may update or delete the user's own Training Assistant workout records. Authentication and coaching records are handled through Fitness with Benjamin's Supabase project. Model processing is provided through the connected AI service under the user's account and its applicable terms.

Users may disconnect FWB Training Assistant through their AI service. To request access, correction, or deletion of Fitness with Benjamin records, email fwb@benjaminbenz.com.

Do not use this service for emergencies or submit information about another person without authorization.
