FWB Training Assistant

FWB Training Assistant is an AI feature for active Fitness with Benjamin clients. After linking your account, it can help you reflect on your program and progress, record complete workouts and check-ins when you ask, correct or undo Training Assistant workout entries, and queue a message for Benjamin.

It is not Benjamin, FWB Coach, medical care, or an emergency service. It does not store your full ChatGPT or Claude conversation.
