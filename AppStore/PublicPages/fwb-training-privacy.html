FWB Training Privacy Notice

Draft for owner review — not yet published.

FWB Training is available to authorized Fitness with Benjamin clients. The app uses an email address to authenticate a client and connect the client to their assigned coaching account.

Depending on the features a client uses, FWB Training processes assigned programs, exercise and cardio logs, workout history, readiness check-ins, progress measurements, and calorie and macro targets. This information is used to provide the app's training and progress features. Authentication and coaching records are handled through Fitness with Benjamin's Supabase project.

The app stores limited preferences and unfinished workout data on the device to support timers, settings, and offline continuity. The app does not use this data for advertising and does not track clients across other companies' apps or websites.

If a client chooses to connect Apple Health, FWB Training writes completed workout type, start and end time, duration, and any distance or calories the client entered. The app does not read information from Apple Health and does not use Health data for advertising, marketing, or data mining. Apple Health access can be managed in iPhone Settings.

Exercise demo links may open YouTube. A client's use of YouTube is governed by YouTube's own terms and privacy practices.

Records are retained while needed to provide coaching services and meet applicable business or legal obligations. To request access, correction, export, or deletion of a Fitness with Benjamin account and associated data, email fwb@benjaminbenz.com. The request may need to be verified before it is completed.

Do not use FWB Training for emergencies or submit another person's information without authorization. Questions about this notice can be sent to fwb@benjaminbenz.com.
