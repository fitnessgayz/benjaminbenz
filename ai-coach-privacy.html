Benjamin AI Coach Privacy Notice

Effective August 12, 2026.

The plugin accesses only the coaching account linked by the user. Depending on the request, it may retrieve coaching goals, program summaries, progress entries, check-ins, and open messages to Benjamin.

The plugin does not intentionally store complete ChatGPT conversations. It stores a structured check-in, progress note, or coach request only when the user asks to save or send it. Authentication and coaching records are handled through Fitness with Benjamin's Supabase project. Model processing is provided through ChatGPT under the user's OpenAI account and applicable OpenAI terms.

Users may disconnect the plugin through ChatGPT. To request access, correction, or deletion of Fitness with Benjamin records, email fwb@benjaminbenz.com.

Do not use this service for emergencies or submit information about another person without authorization.
